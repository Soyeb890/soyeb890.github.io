Private Label / OEM

Private Label Clothing Manufacturing in the UAE

Raqeeb manufactures under your brand, not ours — from an initial sample through to full production, with core stages handled in-house at our Ajman workshop.

Discuss Your Brand WhatsApp
Overview

Production under your own label, not ours

The finished product carries your brand, not ours. Construction, fit, colourways and branding placement follow your specification, produced through the same workshop process used across our uniform and apparel work. Orders start from 10 pieces per design or colour and scale into ongoing production as the requirement grows.

What Raqeeb handles

In-house, under one workshop

01

Sampling

Sampling before any production commitment, so your specification is confirmed before a full run starts.

02

Cutting & stitching

Pattern cutting and production stitching handled on our Ajman workshop floor.

03

Embroidery & printing

In-house embroidery and printing, matched to your artwork and label placement.

04

Finishing

Final finishing and quality checks before an order is dispatched under your brand.

Branding and label requirements are confirmed with the production brief.

Who this suits

Brands producing under their own name

Apparel and workwear brands, corporate and hospitality groups building a branded-wear line, and retailers or distributors sourcing garments to their own specification and label — for a first sample run or an ongoing production program.
