# Raqeeb Website Foundation

This document is the approved strategic and design reference for the Raqeeb Garments Workshop website.

It consolidates the Website Engineering Brief, Website Master Blueprint, and Website Design System into one practical source of truth.

---

## 1. Commercial objective

The website must perform two commercial jobs:

1. Convert legitimate small and medium enquiries, including orders from approximately 10–500 pieces.
2. Build procurement confidence for recurring and high-volume corporate programs, including opportunities in the 10,000–100,000+ piece range.

Primary outcomes:

- Qualified quote requests
- WhatsApp and call enquiries
- Capability-profile requests
- Factory-visit requests
- Corporate RFP conversations
- Organic search discovery for industry-specific manufacturing terms

The website must not become a generic brochure, a catalog-only site, or a template full of unsupported claims.

---

## 2. Audience paths

### Small inbound buyer

Needs to see:

- MOQ clarity
- Product range
- Easy contact
- Fast WhatsApp route

Best path:

Home → Products/Industry → WhatsApp or Quick Quote

### Corporate procurement buyer

Needs to see:

- Production scale
- In-house capability
- Operational proof
- Quality-control process
- Factory credibility
- Clear RFP route

Best path:

Home → Corporate Programs → Capability Profile / Corporate Inquiry / Factory Visit

### Operations and sector decision-makers

Needs to see:

- Industry-specific products
- Fabric and construction relevance
- Lead-time and reorder confidence
- Real production proof

Best path:

Home → Industry Page → Quote / WhatsApp

### Private-label brand

Needs to see:

- Confidentiality
- Sampling
- OEM capability
- Low-to-high volume flexibility
- Full production control

Best path:

Home → Private Label/OEM → Discuss Your Brand

## 5. Messaging system

Core message:

Raqeeb manufactures uniforms and branded apparel in-house in the UAE, with flexible order sizes and complete production control from cutting to finishing.

Messaging pillars:

1. Full in-house control
2. Flexible scale
3. Proven sector experience
4. Long operating history
5. UAE-based manufacturing
6. Private-label/OEM capability

Tone:

- Calm
- Factual
- Direct
- Specific
- Numbers-led
- Confident without hype

Avoid:

- Leading
- Best
- #1
- World-class
- Guaranteed
- Unverified certifications
- Named clients without permission
- Claims that cannot be demonstrated

Use consistently:

- In-house manufacturing
- Uniform program
- Private label / OEM
- Sampling
- Lead time
- Production capacity
- Quality control
- UAE / Ajman

## 6. Proof system

Approved proof categories, subject to final wording confirmation:

- Nearly 20 years of company operation
- Founder with approximately 40 years of industry experience
- Approximately 50–60 specialized workers
- Approximately 95 stitching machines
- Full in-house cutting, stitching, embroidery, printing, and finishing
- Airline uniform programs of approximately 5,000–10,000+ pieces
- Hospitality programs of approximately 6,000+ pieces
- Transport-sector program scaling up to approximately 150,000+ pieces
- Public-sector program of approximately 50,000+ pieces
- Manufacturing work for 100+ private-label brands
- 4,000+ senior jackets produced from cutting through embroidery

Rules:

- Use “approximately” and “up to” where appropriate.
- Never imply official endorsement.
- Never reveal client identity without permission.
- Pair numbers with real factory or production evidence.
- Do not present exceptional maximums as normal order sizes.
- Confirm every proof claim before publication.

## 7. Design philosophy

Brand personality:

- Precise
- Capable
- Unpretentious
- Quietly confident
- Operational rather than promotional

Visual position:

- Industrial with polish
- Engineering-led
- Photography-first
- Minimal structure with rich real-world evidence
- Premium execution without luxury-fashion styling

Core principle:

**We do not decorate. We demonstrate.**

The site should feel like a serious, organized factory—not a reseller, print shop, or generic marketing template.

---

## 8. Design language

Use:

- One neutral, highly legible sans-serif type family
- Consistent spacing scale
- Consistent max-width container and grid
- Small, consistent corner radius
- Flat or lightly bordered cards
- Two button tiers only
- Visible form labels
- Strong focus states
- Structured stat components
- Shallow navigation
- Sticky quote access
- Consistent photo crops and grading
- White/near-white backgrounds with one dark authority tone
- Calm, functional motion only

Avoid:

- Heavy shadows
- Pill-shaped consumer-app styling
- Decorative gradients
- Auto-playing carousels
- Parallax
- Scroll hijacking
- Excessive animation
- Decorative illustration
- Stock handshakes
- Generic office photos
- Many equally weighted CTAs
- Placeholder-only form labels

- GitHub Pages

---

## 13. Safe workflow

Required sequence:

1. Work on a separate branch.
2. Preserve the current live site.
3. Show changed files.
4. Provide preview or screenshots.
5. Test mobile and desktop.
6. Test forms and links.
7. Wait for explicit owner approval.
8. Open a pull request.
9. Never merge into `main` without explicit approval.
10. Verify the live site immediately after launch.

Never casually modify:

- CNAME
- Domain settings
- Form destination
- Redirect behavior
- Contact information
- Existing live assets
- Main branch
