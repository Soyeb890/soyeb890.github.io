Product overview

A versatile staple for corporate and hospitality teamwear

Polo shirts are a straightforward anchor for a uniform programme — easy to coordinate across a team and simple to reorder as headcount grows. Colour, fabric and logo placement are set against your brief, with cutting, stitching, embroidery and printing carried through one production run.

Common applications
Corporate offices, hospitality front-of-house teams, event and promotional programmes, schools and institutions
Fabric
Knit fabric, with the exact weight and finish confirmed at quotation or sampling
Construction / options
Collared, short-sleeve knit shirt, supplied blank or finished with your logo
Branding
Embroidered or printed, positioned to the approved artwork
Color
Matched to your brand or team colour brief
Fit & sizing
Size run built around your team; MOQ from 10 pieces per design or colour
