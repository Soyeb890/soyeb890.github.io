FAQ

Answers to common questions

What's your minimum order?

MOQ starts at 10 pieces per design or color.

Can you do embroidery and printing?

Yes — both are handled in-house, with printing offered alongside embroidery on request.

How long is production?

Production timing depends on the product, quantity and customization requirements. We confirm the timeline with your quotation.

Do you support private label / OEM manufacturing?

Yes. We manufacture under your brand — sampling first, then full production once it's approved.

Do you ship internationally?

International enquiries are welcome. Delivery arrangements, alongside UAE-wide delivery or pickup, are confirmed with your quotation.

Can I visit the workshop?

Yes, workshop visits can be arranged for corporate and procurement enquiries — ask us via WhatsApp, call, or the form below.
