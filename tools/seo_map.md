# SEO Map — Phase 4A

Audit of every indexable page in the V2 build against the search-intent hierarchy
in the phase brief. This document records what was found and what (if anything)
changed. It is a reference artifact, not a template to keep editing page-by-page
outside of a future SEO phase.

Legend: **Change?** — `none` (already correct, left as-is), `title` (brand-suffix
consistency fix only), `new` (new page created this phase).

## Summary

- 21 pages audited (20 indexable + `thanks.html`, which stays `noindex` and out of the sitemap).
- Titles: 6 product pages had a full-brand vs. short-brand ("— Raqeeb" / "| Raqeeb") inconsistency in the suffix; normalized to `Raqeeb Garments Workshop` to match the other 12 pages and the new `og:site_name`. No wording changes beyond the brand suffix.
- Meta descriptions: all already unique, specific, and free of unverified claims. No changes.
- Product-page copy: 15 of 18 product pages had a hero lead paragraph that said "produced in-house" without naming the location (3 already said "in Ajman" — Medical Scrubs, Polo Shirts, Custom T-Shirts). Added "in Ajman" to the same sentence on the other 15 pages so the on-page copy consistently carries the local-SEO signal already present in every meta description. No other wording changed; no new claims added.
- H1s: all already specific and on-intent. No changes made anywhere on the site.
- Structured data (Product / BreadcrumbList / canonical / OG) was already in place on every product page and already followed the "no Offer/price/rating" rule and the T-shirt no-image rule. See the phase final report for the one genuine gap found (`og:site_name` missing site-wide) and how it was fixed.
